# Harbourline Bank — Specialist Escalation and Handover Standard (ESC)
*Fictional policy written for a prototype. Version 1.4, effective 1 May 2026. Owner: Complaints Operations Manager.*

**ESC-1 When to escalate.** Complaints handlers resolve complaints themselves unless one of the following applies, in which case the case must be handed to the named specialist team:
- Suspected fraud, unauthorised transaction or scam → *Fraud & Scams*.
- A lending decision, affordability, mortgage or loan arrears → *Lending Specialists*.
- A vulnerability referral under VUL-4 → *Vulnerable Customer Support*.
- A suspected systemic issue (the same fault affecting many customers), a threat of legal action, or media contact → *Senior Complaints Review*.
- A systems or app failure that needs technical investigation → *Digital Operations*.

**ESC-2 Timing.** Handovers must be made within 2 business days of the trigger being identified (1 business day for scams, see FRD-2).

**ESC-3 Required handover fields.** A handover is complete only when all ten fields are present:
1. *Complaint summary* in plain language.
2. *Date received* and current deadline status (see CH-5).
3. *Products and accounts affected* (account references only, never full card numbers).
4. *Customer's desired outcome*, in their words where possible.
5. *Timeline of events*, dated.
6. *Actions taken so far* by the bank, including any account protection steps.
7. *Evidence held*, listed (statements, call recordings, letters, screenshots).
8. *Vulnerability assessment*: indicators found, or a statement that none were found, and whether the customer was asked.
9. *Contact preference* and any reasonable adjustments.
10. *Open questions* for the specialist.

**ESC-4 Returned handovers.** A specialist may return a handover that is missing any ESC-3 field. The return must state which fields are missing. Returned handovers are tracked as a quality measure for each team.

**ESC-5 Ownership.** The complaints handler remains the customer's point of contact until the specialist confirms acceptance of the handover.
