# Harbourline Bank — Lending and Arrears Policy (LND)
*Fictional policy written for a prototype. Version 3.1, effective 1 February 2026. Owner: Head of Lending Operations.*

**LND-1 Affordability.** Lending decisions (loans, credit cards, overdrafts, mortgages) must be based on a reasonable assessment of affordability. Complaints that a lending decision was unaffordable or irresponsible must be handed to Lending Specialists.

**LND-2 Declined applications.** Customers who are declined must be told the main reason where possible and the credit reference agency used. A complaint about a decline is reviewed by Lending Specialists; complaints handlers must not overturn a lending decision.

**LND-3 Arrears contact.** Customers in arrears must be contacted with forbearance options (payment plans, payment holidays, interest freezes) before any default notice. Contact must not be excessive: no more than one call per day and three per week.

**LND-4 Default and fees in arrears.** Arrears fees must be reviewed where the customer was in financial difficulty (see FEE-4). Default notices issued without first offering forbearance must be reviewed by Lending Specialists.

**LND-5 Mortgage payment changes.** Where a mortgage payment changes, the customer must be given at least 30 days' written notice. Failure to give notice is a communication failure under CH-7 and may require redress under RED.
