# Harbourline Bank — Fraud, Unauthorised Transactions and Scams Policy (FRD)
*Fictional policy written for a prototype. Version 5.0, effective 1 June 2026. Owner: Head of Fraud Operations.*

**FRD-1 Unauthorised transactions.** Where a customer reports a payment they did not make or authorise, the bank must refund the amount by the end of the next business day after the report, unless there are reasonable grounds to suspect the customer acted fraudulently or with gross negligence. Any such suspicion must be recorded by a Fraud & Scams specialist.

**FRD-2 Authorised push payment (APP) scams.** Where a customer was tricked into sending a payment to a fraudster, the case must be handed to the Fraud & Scams team within 1 business day. Reimbursement is assessed by a specialist, not by the complaints handler.

**FRD-3 Required evidence.** A fraud or scam handover must include: the date, time and amount of each disputed payment; the payee details as known; how the customer was contacted by the fraudster (if a scam); whether the customer shared any security details; and the date the customer reported it.

**FRD-4 Customer protection.** On report, the handler must check the account has been secured (cards blocked, online banking credentials reset where needed) and record that this was done.

**FRD-5 No promises.** Handlers must not promise a refund or reimbursement for an APP scam before the specialist decision.
