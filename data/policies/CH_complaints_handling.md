# Harbourline Bank — Complaints Handling Policy (CH)
*Fictional policy written for a prototype. Version 4.1, effective 1 March 2026. Owner: Head of Customer Resolution.*

**CH-1 Definition.** A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction, oral or written, about the bank's products, services or staff, where the customer alleges they have suffered (or may suffer) financial loss, material distress or material inconvenience. A request for information or a change to an account is not a complaint unless dissatisfaction is expressed.

**CH-2 Acknowledgement.** Every complaint must be logged on the day it is received and acknowledged in writing within 3 business days.

**CH-3 Summary resolution.** A complaint resolved to the customer's satisfaction by the end of the third business day after receipt may be closed with a Summary Resolution Communication. The handler must record what was agreed.

**CH-4 Final response deadline.** All other complaints must receive a Final Response within 8 weeks (56 calendar days) of receipt. If the bank cannot issue a Final Response by then, it must send a holding letter explaining why and the customer's right to refer the complaint to the independent ombudsman service.

**CH-5 Deadline risk.** Cases are classed as: *On track* (more than 21 days remaining), *At risk* (8 to 21 days remaining), *Critical* (7 days or fewer remaining) and *Breached* (past the deadline). At risk and Critical cases must be reviewed by a team leader weekly.

**CH-6 Severity.** Severity is set at triage:
- *High*: financial loss over £1,000, suspected fraud or scam, a vulnerable customer at risk of harm, threatened legal action or media contact, or a potential systemic issue affecting many customers.
- *Medium*: financial loss between £100 and £1,000, repeated service failure (the customer has contacted us three or more times about the same issue), or a complaint about staff conduct.
- *Low*: financial loss under £100 or a service issue with no loss.

**CH-7 Root cause.** Every complaint must be tagged with one primary root cause from the approved list: Fees and charges; Unauthorised transaction; Authorised push payment scam; Service delay; Staff conduct; Systems or app failure; Lending decision; Arrears and collections; Account opening or closure; Communication failure.

**CH-8 Fair outcome.** Decisions on whether a complaint is upheld, and on any redress, must be made by an authorised complaints handler or specialist. Decisions must be explained in plain language and recorded with the reasons.

**CH-9 Records.** The case record must contain a timeline of events, all contact with the customer, the evidence considered, and the reasons for the outcome. Records must allow a second person to understand and reproduce the decision.
