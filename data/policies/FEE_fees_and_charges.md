# Harbourline Bank — Fees and Charges Refund Policy (FEE)
*Fictional policy written for a prototype. Version 2.3, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Retail Banking Product Team.*

**FEE-1 Scope.** Applies to fees and charges on current accounts, savings accounts and credit cards, including unarranged overdraft fees, returned payment fees, late payment fees, foreign transaction fees and over-limit fees.

**FEE-2 Bank error.** Where a fee was charged because of a bank error (for example a system fault, an incorrect tariff or a payment the bank failed to process on time), the fee must be refunded in full, together with any interest charged on it, plus a goodwill payment in line with policy RED.

**FEE-3 Discretionary refunds.** Where a fee was correctly charged, a complaints handler may refund up to two fees of the same type in any rolling 12-month period as a goodwill gesture. Further discretionary refunds require team leader approval.

**FEE-4 Financial difficulty.** Where a customer shows signs of financial difficulty, fees charged in the last 6 months should be reviewed and refunded where they have added to the difficulty. The case must also be referred to the Vulnerable Customer Support team under policy VUL.

**FEE-5 Credit card late payment.** A late payment fee on a credit card is correctly charged if the minimum payment was not received by the due date. If the customer set up a Direct Debit and it failed due to a bank error, FEE-2 applies.

**FEE-6 Foreign transaction fees.** Foreign transaction fees are not charged on the Harbourline Travel Card. Any such fee on that card is a bank error and FEE-2 applies.
