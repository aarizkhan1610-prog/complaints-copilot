# Harbourline Bank — Redress and Goodwill Policy (RED)
*Fictional policy written for a prototype. Version 2.0, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Head of Customer Resolution.*

**RED-1 Principle.** Redress aims to put the customer back in the position they would have been in had the error not occurred, and to recognise distress and inconvenience fairly.

**RED-2 Financial loss.** Direct financial loss caused by the bank must be refunded in full, plus 8% simple interest a year for the period the customer was without the money, where that period is more than 30 days.

**RED-3 Distress and inconvenience.** Goodwill payments for distress and inconvenience follow these guide bands:
- *Minor* (short-lived inconvenience, resolved quickly): £25 to £75.
- *Moderate* (repeated contact needed, or disruption over several weeks): £75 to £300.
- *Significant* (substantial distress, impact on health or on essential bills): £300 to £1,000.
Payments above £1,000 require Senior Complaints Review approval.

**RED-4 Authority limits.** Complaints handlers may approve total redress up to £500 per case. Team leaders up to £2,500. Above that, Senior Complaints Review.

**RED-5 No automated decisions.** Redress amounts must be decided and approved by an authorised person. Tools may suggest a band but must not set the amount.
